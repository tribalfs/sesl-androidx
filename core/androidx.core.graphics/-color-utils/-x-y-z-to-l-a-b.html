XYZToLAB

open fun XYZToLAB(@FloatRange(from = 0.0, to = 95.047) x: Double, @FloatRange(from = 0.0, to = 100.0) y: Double, @FloatRange(from = 0.0, to = 108.883) z: Double, @NonNull outLab: Array<Double>)

Converts a color from CIE XYZ to CIE Lab representation.

This method expects the XYZ representation to use the D65 illuminant and the CIE 2° Standard Observer (1931).

  • outLab[0] is L [0, 100]
  • outLab[1] is a [-128, 127)
  • outLab[2] is b [-128, 127)
